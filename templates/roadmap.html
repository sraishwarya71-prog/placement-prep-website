🗺️ Placement Preparation Roadmap

Weeks 1-2: Foundations

Revise core CS subjects (DBMS, OS, CN) and start basic aptitude practice daily.

Weeks 3-5: DSA Practice

Solve 3-5 coding problems/day covering arrays, strings, linked lists, and recursion.

Weeks 6-7: Mock Interviews

Practice HR + technical questions, do at least 2 mock interviews with peers.

Week 8: Resume & Applications

Finalize resume, apply to target companies, and research their hiring process.

Ongoing: Revision

Keep revising weak topics and track your progress weekly.

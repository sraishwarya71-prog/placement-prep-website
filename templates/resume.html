📄 Resume Tips

  • Keep your resume to one page as a fresher.
  • Use action verbs like "built", "led", "optimized" instead of "worked on".
  • Quantify achievements wherever possible (e.g., "reduced load time by 30%").
  • List projects with tech stack and a link (GitHub/live demo) if available.
  • Avoid spelling mistakes — proofread twice.
  • Tailor your resume/keywords to match the job description.
